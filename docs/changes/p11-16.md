## Release notes (player-facing, plain words)
### New

- Town armouries stock eight regional weapons, two horses and the Ostrand Kite. Find the compact horse bow in Merrow and Gullhaven.
- Look for “Made here” in the armoury and open the gear notes on a soldier’s card to find the local arms.

### Changed

- Regional gear can turn up in high-tier loot. Its home markets keep selling it when their towns change hands.

### Under the hood

- The Art gallery shows the new equipment. The longaxe has its own silhouette. The horse bow and arbalest use the kit’s recurve and windlass parts.
- The knight’s lance, longaxe, flats pike and compact horse bow need specialist training. P11-17 adds the masters.

## Measured (for docs/PHASE-11-VALIDATION.md)

- Regional arms: `node scripts/duel-lab.js 12 --regional`, 30 paired matchups, 12 seeds each. All eleven pieces recorded a win and a loss. No balance values changed between the two measurement passes.
- The pike baseline uses the family default profile at the Flats Pike’s damage. The shortbow is the compact horse bow’s mounted baseline.
- `node scripts/duel-lab.js 40`, three pilots. The Arming Sword beat the looter and footman in all runs. Against the huscarl, masher, timer and turtle won 68%, 100% and 100%. P11-00 recorded 100%, 0% and 0% wins by all pilots against those three opponents, respectively.
- `node scripts/parity.js 40`, lone hero, small, close, veterans and outnumbered. Fought wins 100%, 100%, 100%, 100%, 73%. Over-odds +14, +11, +21, +21, +11. P11-00 fought wins were 100%, 100%, 98%, 83%, 78%.
- `node scripts/tactics-lab.js 20 --plans charge,plan:line --hero active --maps open_country`, 22 troops per side. Charge won 30% and lost 72% of allies on average. Line won 65% and lost 48%.
- A high-tier kill has a 0.21% chance to award each piece and a 2.34% chance to award any regional piece.
- `npm test` first passed 1,071 of 1,072 tests. Its sole failure was a profile assertion about family defaults. The assertion now covers item overrides. The full rerun with `node --test --test-concurrency=2 "tests/*.test.js"` passed all 1,072 tests. Lint and typecheck passed with two existing warnings. Playwright’s settlement and gear specs passed all 38 tests across desktop and phone. The gear spec passed all 14 tests again after the ranged comparison fix.
- All 41 troop combat profiles match `main`. Both browser projects played the two armouries and opened the arbalester’s gear note. The phone pass captured Vessara at 812×375.
