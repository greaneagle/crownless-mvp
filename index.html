            Rotate your device

            Crownless plays in landscape. Turn your phone sideways to continue — a battle in progress stays paused until you resume it.

            Settings

            Kept on this device, not in your save.

            The size of the game's text and buttons. Pinch-zoom is kept for the map and battles, so this is how to enlarge the text; it doesn't enlarge the pictures.

            How fast time runs on the map: Normal, an hour a second; Fast, four hours a second. The ▶ button beside the map’s zoom changes it too.

            How many soldiers fight on the field at once, from 20 to 200; the rest wait in reserve and step in as others fall. Lower it if battles stutter on this device. Takes effect from the next battle.

            Large: battles in the open and in forests spread over a field three screens across (more in battles of over 80 at once), and the view follows you. Small: every battle fits on one screen, as before. Takes effect from the next battle.

            Prepare: choose a setup (Balanced, Defensive, Aggressive or your own) before each fight. Go straight in: use your remembered setup without asking.

            Content packs

            A pack adds to the world: places, troops and more. Packs hold data only; nothing in one is ever run. Turning one on or off takes effect on a new game or a reload. A save begun with a pack needs it on to open.
